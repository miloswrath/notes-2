## Summary
An internal overview of business organization and operations, especially how business structures and support functions relate to AI Pioneers.

## Key Points
- Sales teams may be separated by customer needs, such as government customers.
- A **business unit** is a main organizational group, such as vendors, sales, or another core business function.
- **Presales engineering** provides technical expertise and support before a sale.
- Business Operations supports “customer 0”: if TD is selling AI, it should also be using AI internally.
