# Instructions for this repository
---

## Philosophy
---
This structure is meant to serve as a second brain. I propose that the second-brain is made up of three parts that simultaneously support the user in interacting and providing value in the world. 

**Δ (Read)**: This folder is meant to store *knowledge*. This is crystallized knowledge that should be linked and easily searchable - may be comprised of many different parts.
**Θ (write)**: This folder is meant to store *ideas*. Ideas are part of working memory, they are fleeting, they must be written down before other responsiblities consume them. This folder will store ideas of many different types with many different statuses, thus metadata here is incredibly important to organize.
**Ψ (Execute)**: This is the *deployment of knowledge + ideas*. This is ongoing projects, their statuses, and the important links to both crystallized knowledge and ephemeral ideas.

With these three important aspects, one can successfully structure their second brain to serve their unique goals.
