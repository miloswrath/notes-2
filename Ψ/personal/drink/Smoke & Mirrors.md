
> *A smoky, shadowed tribute to my favorite club in Chicago*

### Ingredients

| Ingredient  |                Amount |
| :---------- | --------------------: |
| Mezcal      |                  2 oz |
| Egg white   |                     1 |
| Agave syrup |                  1 oz |
| Cassis      |                3/4 oz |
| Lime        | 1/2 normal-sized lime |

### Method

1. Add all ingredients to a shaker and **dry shake**. *Careful—it gets messy.*
2. Add ice and shake again until well chilled.
3. Double strain into your glass.
4. Finish with bitters.
