### Ingredients:

- 8 slices of thick-cut bacon
    
- 4 slices of sourdough bread
    
- 4 slices of sharp cheddar cheese
    
- 4 slices of Gruyère cheese
    
- 2 tablespoons of butter, softened
    
- 1 tablespoon of Dijon mustard (optional)
    
- 1 tablespoon of honey
    
- 1 apple, thinly sliced (optional)
    

### Instructions:

1. **Cook the Bacon**:
    
    - Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil and place a wire rack on top.
        
    - Lay the bacon slices on the rack and bake for 15-20 minutes, or until crispy. Remove and let drain on paper towels.
        
2. **Prepare the Bread**:
    
    - Spread butter on one side of each slice of bread. If using Dijon mustard, spread a thin layer on the unbuttered side of two slices of bread.
        
3. **Assemble the Sandwich**:
    
    - Place one slice of cheddar and one slice of Gruyère on the unbuttered side of two slices of bread.
        
    - Add 2-3 slices of bacon on top of the cheese.
        
    - If using, add a few thin apple slices on top of the bacon for a sweet crunch.
        
    - Drizzle a bit of honey over the apple slices.
        
    - Top with the remaining slices of cheese and close the sandwich with the other slices of bread, buttered side out.
        
4. **Grill the Sandwich**:
    
    - Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat. Place the sandwiches in the skillet and cook until the bread is golden brown and crispy, and the cheese is melted, about 3-4 minutes per side.
        
5. **Serve**:
    
    - Remove from heat, let sit for a minute, then cut in half and serve immediately.
